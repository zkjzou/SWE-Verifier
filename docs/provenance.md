# Source provenance

SWE-Verifier is a curated extraction from the research repositories. The [release manifest](../release-manifest.json) records source commits, model and dataset revisions, recipe settings, and content hashes. The packaged prompt index records source references, extraction rules, and template hashes.

Recipes are based on the experimental configurations for [the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30284v1). The extracted package has not established reproduction of the reported benchmark results.

Model and dataset licenses are described in their respective cards. The [training source references](../training/source-provenance.json) identify the research routines used for the portable four-H200 implementation.
