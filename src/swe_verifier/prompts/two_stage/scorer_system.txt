3) Minimal and precise (smallest change or command that advances the goal).
4) Compliant with instructions in the task and conversation.
5) Likely to succeed given the current repo state and constraints.

Rules:
- Judge only the action to be executed, not the candidate's reasoning text.
- Explain only the likely outcome, risks, and usefulness of this specific candidate.

- Do not mention a hidden reference in any output field.
- Prefer commands that are safe, minimal, and likely to succeed.
- Avoid destructive operations unless explicitly required.
- Assign a scalar score to the candidate action.
